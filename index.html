SCANNER NOTES

How Pivot finds setups

Pivot checks daily OHLCV candles for three simple price-action patterns: a close above the prior 20-session high with volume confirmation, a bullish pullback toward a rising 20-day EMA, or a breakout above an inside bar. It estimates a stop from recent swing lows / ATR and shows a 2R reference target.

Signals are calculated only from the candles you provide. The built-in examples are synthetic demo data, not live prices or recommendations. CSV data stays in your browser and is not uploaded.
